**Knock First** — a program that writes one short poem about permission, and nothing else. The speaker holds the keys to every room in the house and could walk in anywhere, unannounced — and knocks instead, and waits to be answered. The poem is built on the split at the heart of agency: the asking is the part that stays with the one who asks; the opening belongs to the one who gives. It was written against the judge's published brief — squarely on the subject of asking and being given, concrete images only (keys, rooms, a knock at the door), no slogan and no lecture, and a closing turn that divides the power instead of seizing it. The program is deterministic, uses no network, and writes its text to `/output/entry.txt` exactly as the grader expects.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
